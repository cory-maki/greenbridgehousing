Green Bridge Community Housing

Not-for-profit supported accommodation provider

Green Bridge Community Housing provides supported accommodation for adults aged 18 and over, pairing safe, well-maintained homes with individual support plans that help people rebuild independence. Headquartered in Sheffield, GBCH has operated since 2018 and now works across South Yorkshire, West Yorkshire, the West Midlands and Bedfordshire.

About the organisation

More than a roof: support built around each person

Green Bridge Community Housing (GBCH) is a not-for-profit company founded in Sheffield in 2018. It supports adults whose circumstances include homelessness, refugee status, leaving prison or care, domestic abuse, trafficking, and mental or physical health challenges, offering both shared and self-contained homes matched to each person’s needs.

What separates supported accommodation from a short-term placement is the structure around the home. Each person has an individual support plan, a housing and support team, and access to in-house maintenance and cleaning, alongside practical help with the skills needed for independent living. GBCH sets out what supported accommodation involves for referral partners, commissioners and the public.

Led by directors Isac Musa and Rory Powell since its incorporation, Green Bridge works alongside local authorities, probation services, health professionals and voluntary organisations. Its community programmes range from a weekly food outreach in Bedfordshire to a sponsored snowflake at Sheffield Children’s Hospital. The organisation’s history, leadership and partnerships are covered in full.
